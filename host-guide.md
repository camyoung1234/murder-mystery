## 1. Storyline & True Timeline

### The Premise
Billionaire horologist **Lord Arthur Vance (72)** has invited family, executives, staff, and old rivals to **Ravenshollow Manor** on a stormy November night for his 72nd birthday gala. He announced two bombshells:
1. He will unveil his final clockwork masterpiece, **The Chronos Automaton**, at the 9:00 PM toast.
2. He is signing a **New Will** transferring his fortune to a public transparency foundation—including a full public audit of **Vance Precision Instruments' 2008 medical trials**.

### What Actually Happened (The True Timeline)
- **18 Years Ago (2008):** **Dr. Julian Mercer (`#4`)**, then Chief Resident at *St. Jude's Clinic*, falsified calibration logs (`Log 08-B`, signed *"J.M."*) on a Vance Precision surgical pump to cover up his own fatal dosing error. Arthur Vance discovered the forgery, quietly buried the scandal, and kept Mercer on a leash as his personal physician.
- **2 Days Before the Gala:** Arthur recorded an angry rehearsal/memoir passage onto a blank brass phonograph cylinder for the Chronos Automaton: *"You've gone too far! I'm calling the police as soon as the lines are back!"*
- **Night of the Gala — 7:45 PM:** Chef/Butler decants the 1998 Bordeaux on the **Dining & Sideboard** (a single glass will be carried up to the Study desk) and prepares Arthur's Study water carafe.
- **7:58 PM (Red Herring #1):** **Victor Vance (`#5`)**, drowning in gambling debt, sneaks into the **Dining & Sideboard** and slips **chloral hydrate (sleeping powder)** into Arthur's **1998 Bordeaux decanter**, planning to knock Arthur out later and steal the New Will, then slips into the Study around **8:05 PM** to see where the Will is kept.
- **8:15 PM (The Setup):** **Dr. Julian Mercer (`#4`)** slips into the Study, swaps the Chronos Automaton's gala greeting cylinder for Arthur's angry recorded cylinder, sets it to play at **8:55 PM**, and rigs the Automaton's heavy brass counterweight to release at **9:00 PM**—smashing the water carafe on Arthur's desk to simulate a gunshot/struggle.
- **8:30 PM (Red Herring #2):** **Lady Evelyn Vance (`#1`)** has a furious public argument with Arthur about being disinherited.
- **8:35 PM (The Blackmail):** **Dr. Mercer** corners Chief Engineer **Jasper Moore (`#6`)**—whose initials are *also* **J.M.**—and threatens to expose Jasper's blueprint theft unless Jasper trips the manor's backup generator breaker for 15 seconds at **9:00 PM sharp**.
- **8:40 PM:** Butler **Thomas Graves (`#3`)** pours a piping-hot single cup of chamomile tea in Arthur's Study.
- **8:42 PM (The Murder Weapon):** In the Grand Hall, in plain sight of everyone, **Dr. Mercer** hands Arthur his nightly **8:45 PM Heart Tonic**—a tiny amber glass vial. Tonight, Mercer replaced the digitalis with **concentrated Aconite** (fast-acting, odorless cardiac poison).
- **8:45 PM:** Arthur enters his Study alone, **bolts the oak door from the inside**, and drinks his 8:45 PM Heart Tonic. He never touches his tea or his wine.
- **8:48 PM (Actual Time of Death):** Arthur suffers sudden cardiac arrest, collapses face-down onto his desk blotter, and knocks his **pocket watch** onto the desk corner—shattering its crystal and freezing its hands at **8:48 PM**.
- **8:55 PM (The Illusion):** Guests in the Grand Hall hear Arthur's recorded voice from the Study (*"You've gone too far!..."*). **Dr. Mercer** is standing in the center of the Grand Hall with Evelyn and others, establishing his "airtight" alibi.
- **9:00 PM (The Blackout):** The Grand Astronomical Clock chimes nine. **Jasper Moore (`#6`)** cuts the generator breaker for 15 seconds. Simultaneously, the Automaton's counterweight drops, shattering the **water carafe** (*crack + heavy thud*).
- **9:01 PM (The Cover-Up):** Lights return. Guests break open the bolted Study door. **Dr. Mercer** rushes to the body first, secretly pockets the empty aconite vial, snaps shut Arthur's broken pocket watch (later lying that it always ran 12 minutes slow), and loudly claims he smelled "bitter almonds from the spilled wine" to frame whoever touched the wine.

## 2. The 4-Pillar Logical Deduction Proof

Players can deduce **Dr. Julian Mercer (`#4`)** before the Final Verdict by connecting these **4 fair, concrete pillars**:

| Pillar | What Players Must Connect | Sources |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Time of Death Was 8:48 PM (Not 9:00 PM)** | Arthur's pocket watch stopped at **8:48 PM** (Exhibit B). Mercer claims the watch ran 12 minutes slow—**contradicted** by Arthur's Horologist Journal (Exhibit E: *"My pocket watch is calibrated daily to the naval observatory"*). Butler Graves confirms the single cup of 8:40 PM tea sat untouched and barely lukewarm by 9:02 PM, and Evelyn/Celeste confirm the Chronos Automaton can play recorded brass cylinders and drop counterweights on a timer (Exhibit D). | Exhibit B, Exhibit D, Exhibit E, Evelyn (`#1`), Clara (`#2`), Graves (`#3`), Celeste (`#7`) |
| **2. The Poison Was in the 8:45 PM Tonic (Not the Wine)** | Victor (`#5`) admits he spiked the **wine decanter** with sedative—**not** lethal poison. Exhibit D & Exhibit F prove the wine glass was **full and untouched**, and Arthur died of **concentrated Aconite**, not sedative. Since the door was bolted from inside at 8:45 PM, the only thing Arthur consumed between 8:45 and 8:48 PM was the **Heart Tonic** handed to him by **Dr. Mercer** at 8:42 PM. | Exhibit D, Exhibit F, Graves (`#3`), Victor (`#5`) |
| **3. Mercer's Fatal Dialogue Slip** | In the Act I Opening Script, Mercer claims: *"The moment the lights came on, I saw Arthur's chalk-pale lips and smelled bitter almonds from the spilled wine."* **Two fatal lies** exposed in Act II: (1) Exhibit D's Study Re-Inspection proves the shattered vessel was a **water carafe** (clear, odorless puddle), not wine; (2) Arthur was slumped **face-down** on his desk blotter—nobody could see his lips until Mercer rolled him over! | Act I Opening Script, Exhibit D, Exhibit F |
| **4. The "J.M." Motive Trap** | Exhibit C shows a charred 2008 St. Jude's Clinic audit log falsified by **"J.M."** Most groups will suspect **Jasper Moore (`#6`)** because his initials are J.M. and he tripped the breaker. **However**, Exhibit E (Employment Ledger) proves Jasper wasn't hired until **2012** (he was in college in 2008)—whereas **Dr. Julian Mercer (`#4`)** was Chief Resident at St. Jude's Clinic in **2008**! | Exhibit C, Exhibit E, Clara (`#2`), Jasper (`#6`), Mercer (`#4`) |

## 4. Werewolf Voting, Detention & Blackout Rules

### Town Hall Votes (End of Act I & Act II — "Search & Detain" — 3 Minutes Each)
1. **Rapid Debate (2 minutes):** Everyone gathers in the main room to share findings and make accusations.
2. **Quick Vote (1 minute):** Every non-detained, non-intimidated player votes for **one suspect** to **Search & Detain** (fast simultaneous point-on-3, paper slip, or Digital Companion). Ties are broken by a 30-second runoff between tied suspects (if still tied, no one is detained).
3. **Consequences of Detention:**
   - The Detained player is **NOT eliminated**—they stay in the room, keep talking, and complete all mandatory dialogues!
   - Their **Sealed Detention Card** is immediately opened and **read aloud to the entire room**, exposing a major secret or clue.
   - They **lose their vote** in the *next* Town Hall (they regain their vote for the Final Verdict in Act III!) and **cannot act** during the upcoming Blackout Phase.

### Blackout Phases (Between Act I→II and Act II→III — 1 Minute Each)
Dim the lights and have **everyone close their eyes** (and lightly tap their knees/table to mask footsteps/rustling). The Host narrates briskly:

1. **Shadow Team Wakes (25 sec):** *"Shadow Team, open your eyes. Silently recognize each other."* (In Blackout #1, this is how the Killer and Accomplices confirm each other.) *"If Dr. Mercer is not Detained, point silently to ONE guest to **Intimidate** (they will be Shaken and lose their vote in the next Town Hall) — OR hold up TWO fingers to **Plant a False Lead** on the Evidence Board."* (Host acknowledges silently.) *"Shadow Team, close your eyes."*
2. **The Inspector Wakes (25 sec):** *"Inspector Harriet Vance-Cole, if you are not Detained, open your eyes. Point silently to ONE player whose Sealed Detention Card you wish to inspect."* Host quietly shows Harriet that player's Detention Card for 10–15 seconds, then takes it back. *"Inspector, close your eyes."*
3. **Lights Up (10 sec):** *"Everyone, open your eyes!"* If someone was Intimidated, the Host announces: *"[Name] was cornered in the dark hallway by an unseen figure and threatened—they are **Shaken** and cannot vote in the next Town Hall."* If a False Lead was planted, the Host places one **Red Herring Slip** on the Evidence Board.

### Final Verdict (End of Act III — "Indictment" — 3 Minutes)
Every player (including Detained and Shaken players!) submits a final ballot with:
1. **The Killer** *(Determines Team Victory)*
2. **The Accomplice(s)** *(Bonus)*
3. **How & Why It Was Done** *(Bonus — Master Detective Award)*

### Win Conditions
- **Innocent Guests Win** if a plurality/majority of Final Verdict ballots indict **Dr. Julian Mercer (`#4`)**.
- **Shadow Team Wins** if the Final Verdict indicts **anyone else** (including if they successfully frame Jasper Moore or Victor Vance!).

## 5. Round-by-Round Host Script (30–45 Minute Pacing)

> **Target Runtime: ~40 Minutes** (flexes naturally between 30–45 min)
> - **Setup & Prologue:** 4 min *(1 min sheet skim + 2 min Prologue + 1 min Act I Opener)*
> - **Act I:** 11 min *(7 min mingle + 3 min Town Hall Vote #1 + 1 min Blackout #1)*
> - **Act II:** 11 min *(1 min Opener + 6 min mingle + 3 min Town Hall Vote #2 + 1 min Blackout #2)*
> - **Act III & Reveal:** 14 min *(1 min Opener + 7 min mingle + 3 min Final Verdict + 3 min Grand Reveal)*

### PROLOGUE (Read Aloud to Start the Game — 2 Min)
> *"Welcome to Ravenshollow Manor. Outside, a November nor'easter has washed out the wooden bridge to the mainland, and the telephone lines are dead. You are all guests of Lord Arthur Vance, the eccentric billionaire clockmaker celebrating his 72nd birthday.*
>
> *Tonight, Arthur promised two announcements at 9:00 PM: the unveiling of his final masterpiece, the **Chronos Automaton**, and the signing of a **New Will** leaving his entire empire to a transparency foundation.*
>
> *At 8:45 PM, Arthur retired alone to his second-floor Study to fetch the Will, bolting the heavy oak door from the inside. At 8:55 PM, several of you in the Grand Hall heard Arthur's voice through the Study door, shouting furiously: 'You've gone too far! I'm calling the police as soon as the lines are back!'*
>
> *At 9:00 PM sharp, as the Grand Astronomical Clock struck nine, the backup generator died—plunging the manor into pitch darkness for fifteen seconds. In the dark, a sharp crack and a heavy thud echoed from the Study. When the lights flickered back on at 9:01 PM, you broke down the bolted Study door—and found Lord Arthur Vance dead at his desk.*
>
> *No one can leave until the storm clears at dawn. One of you is a murderer—and they are not working alone."*

> *You thought Lord Arthur Vance died at 9:00 PM during the blackout. That was the illusion.*
>
> *Arthur Vance died at **8:48 PM**—alone behind a bolted door—killed by **concentrated Aconite** hidden inside his nightly **8:45 PM Heart Tonic**. Victor Vance did spike the wine, yes—with harmless sleeping powder—and Arthur never touched a drop of it. The shattered glass on the floor was his water carafe, smashed at 9:00 PM by the Chronos Automaton's rigged brass counterweight, five minutes after the Automaton played a **pre-recorded brass cylinder** of Arthur's angry voice.*
>
> *And why did the killer claim Arthur's 8:48 PM pocket watch ran twelve minutes slow? Because Arthur's own journal proves his watch never lost a second. Why did the killer claim they saw Arthur's pale lips and smelled bitter almonds from spilled wine the moment the lights came on? Because the puddle was odorless water, and Arthur was slumped face-down!*
>
> *Everyone assumed 'J.M.' on the 2008 St. Jude's Clinic fraud log was Chief Engineer **Jasper Moore**—especially since Jasper tripped the generator breaker at 9:00 PM. Jasper WAS an accomplice, blackmailed over stolen blueprints—but Jasper wasn't even hired until 2012.*
>
> *In 2008, there was only one 'J.M.' at St. Jude's Clinic: the man who handed Arthur his 8:45 PM Heart Tonic in plain sight, and then pronounced him dead.*
>
> ***Dr. Julian Mercer.***"
