Always Visible — Public Facts

Known Sequence of Events (Nov 19)

  • 8:45 PM: Lord Arthur Vance retires alone to his second-floor Study to retrieve his unsigned New Will, bolting the heavy oak door from the inside.
  • 8:55 PM: Guests in the Grand Hall hear Arthur's voice shouting from upstairs: "You've gone too far! I'm calling the police as soon as the lines are back!"
  • 9:00 PM Sharp: As the Grand Astronomical Clock chimes nine, the backup generator cuts out—plunging the manor into total darkness for 15 seconds. A sharp crack and heavy thud echo from the Study.
  • 9:01 PM: Lights return. Guests force open the bolted Study door and find Lord Arthur Vance dead at his desk.
