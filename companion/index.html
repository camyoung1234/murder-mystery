The Truth Behind the Last Chime

The Killer: Dr. Julian Mercer (#4)
The Accomplices (Shadow Team):

Arthur Vance did not die at 9:00 PM. He died at 8:48 PM—alone behind a bolted door—from concentrated Aconite slipped into his nightly 8:45 PM Heart Tonic (handed to him by Dr. Mercer in plain sight at 8:42 PM).

Victor Vance spiked the red wine with harmless sleeping powder, which Arthur never touched. The 8:55 PM argument was a pre-recorded brass wax cylinder played by the Chronos Automaton, and the 9:00 PM crash was the Automaton's brass counterweight smashing the water carafe while blackmailed Chief Engineer Jasper Moore (#6) cut the generator breaker for 15 seconds.

The 4-Pillar Proof: (1) Arthur's pocket watch froze at 8:48 PM and his Horologist Journal proves it never lost a second; (2) The wine was untouched and Arthur died of odorless Aconite from his 8:45 PM tonic; (3) Mercer slipped up in Act I by claiming he saw Arthur's pale lips (Arthur was face-down!) and smelled bitter almonds from spilled wine (the puddle was odorless water!); (4) Jasper Moore wasn't hired until 2012—making Dr. Julian Mercer the only "J.M." at St. Jude's Clinic in 2008!
