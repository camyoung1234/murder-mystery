Town Hall Vote — Public "Search & Detain"

All Town Hall votes are public. At the end of Act I, publicly vote to Detain 1 suspect AND elect 1 Group Leader (who breaks all detention ties and picks 1 additional player to Detain). Detained players remain in the game, read their Sealed Detention Card aloud, and skip their next Town Hall vote.

Final Verdict — Formal Indictment

Every player (including Detained players) casts 1 final public indictment vote on the Town Hall tab. Click Check Verdict Result to see which team wins!
