# Core Cast Dossiers — Players `#1` to `#8` (Required for All Games)

> **Host Instructions:** Give each player ONLY their own dossier. Cut out or fold each player's **Sealed Detention Card** into a separate envelope labeled with their name—do NOT let them open or read it aloud unless they are **Detained** in a Town Hall Vote (or inspected by Harriet `#8` during a Blackout).

## CHARACTER `#1`: LADY EVELYN VANCE
**Role:** Arthur's Niece & Disinherited Heiress | **Alignment:** Innocent

### Backstory & Public Persona
You are 36, sharp-tongued, glamorous, and spent the last decade running Arthur's charitable galas expecting to inherit Ravenshollow Manor. Tonight, Arthur told you he was leaving everything to a transparency foundation instead. At **8:30 PM**, you had a furious shouting match with him in the Grand Hall where you said, *"You'll regret this before the clock strikes nine!"* You look terribly guilty—and you know it.

### Your Secret
At **8:50 PM**, you snuck halfway up the stairs toward the Study to eavesdrop on Arthur, hoping to hear who he was leaving his fortune to. You heard **total silence** from the Study at 8:50 PM, then hurried back down before anyone saw you. You were standing right next to **Dr. Julian Mercer (`#4`)** in the Grand Hall at **8:55 PM** when Arthur's angry voice suddenly boomed from upstairs!

### Crucial Knowledge You Hold
- Two days ago, you walked past Arthur's Study while he was recording an angry speech onto a **blank brass phonograph cylinder** for his memoirs: *"You've gone too far! I'm calling the police as soon as the lines are back!"* (You won't connect this until **Act II** when Exhibit D reveals the scratched cylinder!)

### Mandatory Dialogues
- **Act I — Say to Victor Vance (`#5`):** *"Don't play innocent with me, Victor. I saw you slipping out of Arthur's Study at 8:05 PM looking like you'd seen a ghost. What were you doing in there?"*
- **Act II — Say to Celeste Moreau (`#7`):** *(After Exhibit D is revealed)* *"Wait a second, Celeste—a brass wax cylinder?! Two days ago I heard Arthur dictating that exact phrase—'You've gone too far! I'm calling the police'—onto a blank cylinder! Could the Automaton have played it?!"*
- **Act III — Say to Thomas Graves (`#3`):** *"Thomas, think back carefully! Dr. Mercer stood right beside me in the Grand Hall at 8:55 PM—almost like he WANTED me to remember he was there. Did you see Mercer give Arthur anything before Arthur went upstairs?"*

### SEALED DETENTION CARD (Open Only If Detained)
> **Searched from Lady Evelyn's Clutch:** A crumpled draft of a furious letter to Arthur reading *"I won't let you ruin this family tonight,"* PLUS a tiny note in Evelyn's handwriting: *"8:50 PM — Stood on the upper stairs outside Arthur's Study. Complete silence inside. Not a sound until 8:55 PM."*

## CHARACTER `#2`: CLARA STERLING

### Backstory & Public Persona
You are 44, razor-sharp, composed, and have been Arthur Vance's personal attorney since **2005**. You drafted the **New Will** that Arthur was supposed to sign at 9:00 PM. You have zero financial motive—Arthur paid you handsomely either way—so you are the room's natural anchor for facts and timelines.

### Your Secret
You advised Arthur **against** publishing the **2008 St. Jude's Clinic Medical Trial Audit** in his transparency foundation because you knew it would destroy someone's life and put a target on Arthur's back. Arthur refused to listen.

### Crucial Knowledge You Hold
- You know Arthur's New Will specifically mandated releasing **Vault Ledger #4 (2008 St. Jude's Clinic Surgical Pump Trial)**.
- In **Act III**, you help Harriet unlock **Exhibit E** (showing Jasper Moore wasn't hired until 2012, and Arthur's pocket watch never lost a second).

### Mandatory Dialogues
- **Act I — Say to Dr. Julian Mercer (`#4`):** *"Julian, you've been Arthur's doctor for years. His heart was fine at dinner—and I saw you hand him his 8:45 PM Heart Tonic at 8:42 PM. Are you certain his death was poison from the wine?"*
- **Act II — Say to Jasper Moore (`#6`):** *(After Exhibit C is revealed)* *"Jasper, look at this charred audit log from 2008. 'Falsified by J.M.' You're the Chief Engineer—those are your initials, and the lights went out on your watch. Explain yourself!"*
- **Act III — Say to Dr. Julian Mercer (`#4`):** *(After Exhibit E is revealed)* *"Julian, you told the whole room Arthur's pocket watch always ran twelve minutes slow. Yet Arthur's own journal says it hasn't lost half a second in eleven years. And you were Chief Resident at St. Jude's Clinic in 2008. Care to explain?"*

### SEALED DETENTION CARD (Open Only If Detained)
> **Searched from Clara's Briefcase:** A confidential memo from Arthur dated yesterday: *"Clara — Do NOT remove the 2008 St. Jude's clause. The doctor has had eighteen years of my silence; my conscience won't wait another day."* *(Note: Arthur doesn't write the doctor's name, only "the doctor"!)*

## CHARACTER `#3`: THOMAS GRAVES

### Backstory & Public Persona

### Your Secret

### Crucial Knowledge You Hold

- At **8:42 PM**, you personally watched **Dr. Julian Mercer (`#4`)** hand Arthur his nightly **sealed amber Heart Tonic vial** in the Grand Hall, which Arthur always drinks at 8:45 PM sharp.
- At **9:02 PM**, you saw Arthur's smashed pocket watch frozen at **8:48 PM** right before Mercer snapped the lid shut.

### Mandatory Dialogues
- **Act I — Say to Celeste Moreau (`#7`):** *"Forgive me, Miss Moreau, but I saw you on the upstairs gallery at 8:20 PM—well before the gala toast. Did you see anyone else near Lord Arthur's Study?"*
- **Act II — Response to Victor Vance (`#5`)** *(or initiate if Victor hasn't asked)*: *"The wine decanter? Sir, the wine glass on his desk was completely full. What troubles me is the chamomile tea I poured at 8:40 PM—by 9:02 PM, the pot was stone cold."*
- **Act III — Response to Lady Evelyn (`#1`)**: *"Yes, Miss Evelyn. At precisely 8:42 PM, I saw Dr. Mercer hand Lord Arthur his nightly sealed amber Heart Tonic vial in the Grand Hall. Lord Arthur carried it straight up to the Study and locked the door."*

### SEALED DETENTION CARD (Open Only If Detained)
> **Searched from Graves's Coat Pocket:** A butler's service log for tonight: *"8:40 PM — Hot chamomile tea delivered to Study. 8:42 PM — Dr. Mercer hands Lord Arthur his 8:45 PM Heart Tonic vial. 9:02 PM — Teapot stone cold; watch crystal smashed at 8:48 PM."*

## CHARACTER `#4`: DR. JULIAN MERCER — **THE KILLER (SHADOW TEAM)**
**Role:** Arthur's Personal Physician | **Alignment:** **SHADOW TEAM (KILLER)**

> **SECRET ROLE:** You murdered Lord Arthur Vance! Your goal is to **avoid being indicted in the Final Verdict** (ideally by framing **Jasper Moore `#6`** or **Victor Vance `#5`**). You are allied with **Jasper Moore (`#6`)** (and at 14+ players, Damien Cross `#14`; at 18+ players, Nadia Vance `#18`). During Blackout Phases, you wake up with your Shadow Team to **Intimidate** a guest or **Plant a False Lead**.

### What You Actually Did
- In **2008**, as Chief Resident at *St. Jude's Clinic*, you falsified **Calibration Log 08-B ("J.M.")** after a fatal dosing error. Arthur covered it up to keep you as his private doctor—until he decided to publish the 2008 audit in his New Will.
- At **8:15 PM**, you swapped the Chronos Automaton's brass cylinder for Arthur's angry memoir recording (set for **8:55 PM**) and shifted the counterweight to smash the water carafe at **9:00 PM**.
- At **8:35 PM**, you blackmailed Chief Engineer **Jasper Moore (`#6`)** (whose initials are *also* J.M.!) into cutting the generator breaker at **9:00 PM**.
- At **8:42 PM**, you handed Arthur his nightly **8:45 PM Heart Tonic**—laced with **concentrated Aconite**. He died at **8:48 PM**. You stood in the Grand Hall with Evelyn at 8:55 PM to build an airtight alibi, then pocketed the empty vial at 9:01 PM and lied about the watch and "spilled wine."

### Strategy Tips
- Stay calm, helpful, and clinical! Don't over-defend yourself—let people suspect Victor (the wine!) and Jasper (the breaker + "J.M."!).
- If cornered in Act III about St. Jude's 2008 or the watch, claim: *"Arthur told me privately his watch was running slow yesterday—and anyone could have forged 'J.M.' to frame me or Jasper! Look at who actually cut the power!"*

### Mandatory Dialogues
- **Act I — Response to Clara Sterling (`#2`):** *"Arthur's heart was under terrible stress from the family arguments, Clara. And yes, I gave him his usual sealed tonic at 8:42—straight from the pharmacy seal. No, it was definitely fast-acting poison in the wine; I smelled bitter almonds the moment we broke the door down."*
- **Act II — Response to Harriet Vance-Cole (`#8`):** *"I rolled Arthur over to check his pulse—standard medical reflex, Harriet! As for his tonic vial, he always tosses the empty glass in the bin. Honestly, look at Exhibit C—'J.M.'—and ask Jasper why the lights conveniently died at nine."*
- **Act III — Response to Clara Sterling (`#2`):** *"I was a junior resident at St. Jude's eighteen years ago, Clara—hardly in charge of calibration logs! And if Arthur's watch really stopped at 8:48, then he must have been alive and arguing with someone at 8:55 after dropping it. Don't let a clever frame-up fool you."*

### SEALED DETENTION CARD (Open Only If Detained)
> **Searched from Dr. Mercer's Medical Bag:** A small velvet-lined leather case with slots for **six** amber Heart Tonic vials—**five** are sealed and full, and the **sixth slot is empty**. Tucked inside the lining is a faded **2008 St. Jude's Clinic ID badge** reading: *"Dr. Julian Mercer — Chief Resident."*

## CHARACTER `#5`: VICTOR VANCE

### Backstory & Public Persona
You are 39, charming, desperate, and owe \$400,000 to dangerous creditors. You desperately needed Arthur's inheritance—and when you learned he was signing the New Will at 9:00 PM, you panicked.

You didn't want to kill him—you just wanted him to fall asleep before 9:00 PM so you could steal the New Will! When Arthur turned up dead of poison, you were **terrified** you accidentally overdosed him.
- *(Note: Once Exhibit F reveals Arthur never drank the wine and died of Aconite, you realize you are innocent—and you MUST confess to the sleeping powder in Act III so people stop wasting their vote on you!)*

Chloral hydrate! Not Aconite! And the lab note proves he NEVER EVEN DRANK THE WINE! I'm a thief, not a murderer!"*

### SEALED DETENTION CARD (Open Only If Detained)

## CHARACTER `#6`: JASPER MOORE — **THE ACCOMPLICE (SHADOW TEAM)**
**Role:** Chief Engineer at Vance Precision | **Alignment:** **SHADOW TEAM (ACCOMPLICE)**

> **SECRET ROLE:** You are **Dr. Julian Mercer's (`#4`) blackmailed Accomplice**! You win ONLY if **Dr. Mercer escapes indictment** in the Final Verdict. During Blackout Phases, you wake up with Dr. Mercer (and any other Shadow Team members) to coordinate sabotage.

### What You Actually Did
- You have been secretly selling Vance Precision clockwork blueprints to a rival CEO. Last week, while **Dr. Mercer** was treating a cut on your hand in the workshop, he saw the stolen blueprints and threatened to turn you in unless you did one favor tonight: **cut the manor's backup generator breaker for 15 seconds at 9:00 PM sharp.**
- You didn't know Mercer was going to *murder* Arthur—you thought he just wanted to steal the New Will in the dark! Now you're trapped: if Mercer goes down, he'll drag you down with him as an accessory to murder.
- **The "J.M." Trap:** Your initials are **J.M.**, just like Julian Mercer! In Act II, people will accuse you of being the "J.M." on the 2008 St. Jude's log. **Defend yourself** using the truth (you weren't hired until 2012!)—ironically, you want to draw *just enough* confusion without getting yourself convicted, OR redirect suspicion onto Victor (`#5`) and Evelyn (`#1`)!

### Mandatory Dialogues
- **Act I — Response to Harriet Vance-Cole (`#8`):** *"I wasn't in the Grand Hall at 9:00 because I heard a shutter banging loose near the basement stairs, Harriet. Maybe ask why YOU were hovering by the Study door with your nose in Arthur's business!"*

- **Act III — Response to Celeste Moreau (`#7`):** *"Look, Celeste, I know how the Automaton works, sure—I'm an engineer! But I didn't touch it tonight, and I don't know a thing about poison! If you're looking for someone who wanted Arthur silenced, look at the family fighting over his billions!"*

### SEALED DETENTION CARD (Open Only If Detained)

Fifteen seconds of dark, or your blueprint theft goes to the police."* *(Unsigned!)*
